Collective action is defined as any action taken by the coalition as a whole. They can be a motion to have the coalition send a request letter to a company, a motion to change an existing governance document, a motion to remove a member from the coalition delegation, and so on. Approved actions hold the power to do anything to and for the coalition.

To prevent coalition actions from becoming overwhelming and hard to keep up with, there must never be more than 1 collective action proposal introduced to the coalition for debate per week, with a maximum of 4 coalition action items in debate at once. 

In order to take collective action, a member must start by creating a post in the discussion forum. Any member can start a new discussion to gather consensus on an issue. Once that member is ready to propose the collective action plan, this is what happens next:
- The proposal author must notify a coalition delegate along with:
	- A brief summary of the contents of the proposal, and its merits.
		- If the proposal is for external action, the request should mention what the cause of action is, who we are taking action against, the current state of any negotiations, and why this new action is warranted
		- If the proposal is for a procedure change, the request should include an open PR to the Governance GitHub repository, found here: https://github.com/Minecraft-Modder-Coalition/Governance/
		- If the proposal is for member removal, delegation removal, or presidential removal, the request should say who is being targetted, why the action is necessary, whether other courses of action have been taken or considered, if not why not.
			- The motion should also note, in the case of removing the president, whether the president should stay on as a delegate or also be removed.
	- Coalition delegates will verify that the proposal has been discussed and has at least 1 other member in agreement with its contents before it can be brought to a vote of the full coalition
- A coalition delegate will send a new message in the coalition announcements discord channel, where the delegate will include the summary, and a link to view the full proposed action (GitHub PR or Google Doc).
- The proposal will enter a 1 week debate period, where all coalition members are notified of the proposal and can request an amendment be made. How and if its implemented is left at the discretion of the proposal author.
	- At any time, the proposal author can choose to table discussion of the action item should the author wish to continue working on it before bringing it back up for discussion. 
	- The proposal author can also extend the discussion time, should the author feel there is still more discussion needed. After 4 weeks of debate, further extensions requires approval from a majority of the delegation.
	- If the delegates decide, by a majority vote, that the final proposal is substantially different from the original proposal, a coalition delegate must extend debate on the proposal for 1 week. The delegate should notify the coalition of the new proposal draft upon extending debate.
- Once debate has concluded, a coalition delegate should setup a vote to gather consensus and further edits to the proposal are not permitted. For a vote to pass, at least 50% of members must participate[^2]. Members may vote yes, no, or present. Present votes count toward participation but not toward the result. The proposal passes if more than half of the yes and no votes are yes.[^1] Voting for a proposal takes place online over secret ballot on https://vote.heliosvoting.org.
- If a vote passes, the coalition delegation is tasked with the implementation within 48 hours of approval:
	- In the case of a procedural change, they must approve the PR and merge it into the governance documents
	- In the case of a collective action request, they must organize the coalition to take that step of collective action.
		- If the coalition is sending a letter, they must make an announcement for members to sign the letter and either send the letter, in private, to the relevant party, or publish it on the coalition website (or other public platform should that be unavailable)
		- Proposals for open letters or collective action should include whether the coalition should announce the actions its taking on any social media platforms.
	- In the case of a removal, the President should notify the person being removed and then execute the removal.
- If a vote passes that removes the current president, the president is required to relinquish control to the new acting president within 48 hours. This includes control over all services and products owned by the coalition but managed by the president on behalf of the coalition. This includes the Discord server, the GitHub organization, social media accounts, hosting services and so on.
	- Election procedures should commence as stated in the leadership governance document. Transfer of power happens immediately upon the completion of the vote, there will never be a time where the coalition does not have a President, Acting President, or President Pro Tempore.
- In the event that there are no delegates to take action on coalition action procedures, the Acting President may do so in their stead. Any action that requires a majority vote of the delegation may also be decided by the Acting President, but can be overridden by a vote of simple majority from the coalition.

[^1]: We may want to consider different thresholds for different actions

[^2]: Thresholds may need to be progressively smaller at larger sizes, or increase voting times at larger sizes. Or maybe no quorum at all. Need to discuss
