# Who Are We?

We're a group of Minecraft modders aiming to represent all modders in a union-like, especially in topics that concern us modders in opposition to other groups in the modded Minecraft ecosystem.

# What Do We Represent? What Are Our Values?

- Transparency: We want the different actors in the community to be open and transparent about their various concerns, notably about their financial concerns.
- Fairness: We want to strike a good and fair balance between the different actors in the community.
- Quality: We want to encourage quality in modding, and strive to improve. To that end, we explicitly take a stance against AI in modding, whether code or assets or both. We also oppose other forms of low-quality mods, for example various mods that just wrap a datapack for no reason; and we encourage people to hold themselves and others to these quality standards.

## How Do I Contribute?
For now, just talk in the appropriate channels. More nuanced ways of contributing will be set up soon.

## What Are The Implications Of Me Being in the Discord?
None by themselves, proposals will be voted on individually. That said, we hope/expect that you will respect majority decisions, even if you don't agree with them. 

# How do I qualify to join?

To qualify as a member of the coalition, you must:
- Agree to uphold our values as stated in ⁠faq.
- Be a creator of Minecraft Mods across one or more mods combined that have:
    - 100k downloads on CurseForge
    - or 25k downloads on Modrinth
- Attest that you aren't:
    - Employed by a Minecraft Mod hosting website (like CurseForge or Modrinth)
    - Employed by a Minecraft Server Hoster (like Bisect or Akliz)
    - A member of the media / a youtuber
    - A creator who makes primarily modpacks

Members who do not qualify according to the standards above may seek to join via a referral from a current coalition member in good standing.
